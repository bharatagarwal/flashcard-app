## Exact prompt

Create the final square iOS app icon artwork for Recur, a focused traditional flashcard app for learning mathematics. Premium, restrained, beautifully proportioned flat dimensional icon. A single bold ivory flashcard, slightly tilted, overlapping a second pale mint flashcard behind it, with a simple dark evergreen curved turning arrow cut into or drawn on the front card. The arrow means flipping a card and recurring review. Rich deep evergreen background, very subtle lighting and soft contact shadow, crisp carefully rounded card edges. Large clear silhouette readable at 60 pixels. No text, no letters, no numbers, no mathematical notation, no spiral, no ornate detail, no additional objects. Full-bleed 1024x1024 square background, no outer rounded-square mask, no mockup, no border, no watermark. The design must feel like a considered Apple app icon, tactile but mostly flat; warm ivory, fresh mint and deep green.
